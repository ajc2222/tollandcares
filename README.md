# Tolland Cares

Scroll-driven community story using React and Vinext, with a 15-second Seedance 2.0 illustrative film.

## Development
Node 22.13+ and pnpm. Run `pnpm install`, then `pnpm dev`. Production: `pnpm build`.

## Story and counters
Scene timing and copy live in `app/page.tsx`. The paused video seeks with scroll; text follows decoded video time. Doorway transitions leave room for the footage.

Counter digits are illustrative, hidden from assistive technology, and fade before reaching a final amount. The enduring claims supplied by the owner are “Tens of thousands of dollars raised” and “Dozens of families helped.” Do not treat animation values as verified totals.

Mobile preserves the complete widescreen picture above text. Reduced-motion visitors receive a static scene and impact summary. Video errors retain a poster and accessible summary.

`public/care-journey.mp4` is optimized for seeking. The film is AI-generated illustration, not footage of actual projects.
